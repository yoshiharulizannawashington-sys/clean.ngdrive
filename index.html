Foundation status

Milestone 1 — what is real, and what is not.

  • Fixed 120 Hz simulation in a Web Worker, interpolated rendering
  • Rotating engine with inertia, idle governor, rev limiter, stalling and restart
  • Slipping clutch with launch modulation and torque-interrupted shifts
  • Open, viscous, clutch-type, geared and locking differentials · RWD, FWD, AWD
  • Ten surface materials with separate longitudinal/lateral grip and rolling resistance
  • Four raycast suspension units, combined-slip tires, weight transfer and body roll
  • Chase, close, hood, cockpit and free cameras · keyboard and gamepad
  • Soft-body node/beam deformation — not implemented (Phase 3)
  • Mechanical damage and failures — not implemented (Phase 4)
  • Traffic, weather, audio — not implemented (Phases 8–10)
